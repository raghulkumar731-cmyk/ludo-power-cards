Privacy Policy for Ludo: Power Cards

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Ludo: Power Cards ("the app", "we", "us") is an Android game developed by Rahul Kumar. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and your choices.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: if you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from your Google account.
  • Game data: your display name, level, XP, coins, win/loss record and match results.
  • Online play data: room codes and basic in-match information needed to run multiplayer games with other players.
  • Device and usage data: advertising ID, device type, and app usage information collected by our ad and analytics partners.

2. How we use information

  • To sign you in and save your game progress.
  • To run online matches and show player profiles to other players in the match.
  • To show ads and give rewards for watching rewarded ads.
  • To keep the app secure, fix bugs and improve the game.

3. Third-party services

The app uses the following third-party services, which may collect information as described in their own policies:

  • Google Play Services / Google Sign-In
  • Firebase (authentication and database)
  • Google AdMob (advertising)

4. Advertising

The app shows banner, interstitial and rewarded ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may use your advertising ID to show ads. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

5. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Your display name and profile picture are visible to other players in the same match. We share data only with the service providers listed above, or if required by law.

6. Data retention and deletion

We keep your game data for as long as your account exists. To request deletion of your account and data, email us at the address below with the subject "Delete my data". We will delete your data within a reasonable time.

7. Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the date updated above.
