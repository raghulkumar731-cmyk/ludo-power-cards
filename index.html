🪄 Create Your Own Card
Describe the card you want in your own words. The AI will match it to one of the game's real effects and design it for you — name, icon and color included.
Power cards
🛡️ Shield — pick a token, protected from capture for the rest of the game.
👻 Invisible — pick a token, safe from capture through opponents' next 3 rolls. If an opponent uses it, that token vanishes from your view for 45 seconds!
🔀 Teleport — instantly swap one of your tokens with an opponent's token on the main track.
✨ Clone — pick one of your tokens to duplicate onto its own square, linked to it. If the original is ever attacked, it escapes by swapping places with the clone (which is consumed). If the clone itself is attacked or its 45 seconds run out, it vanishes — but if it had pulled ahead of the original by then, the original keeps that lead first.
🍀 Luck — your next dice roll is fixed to land you a capture, if one's possible.

💣 Cannon — snipe an enemy token 1-6 tiles from any of your tokens; it falls back to half its distance.
⭐ Star Smasher — permanently cracks one star (safe zone) tile — it can be captured on from then on.
🕳️ Trap — hide traps on 4 tiles; the first enemy token to land on each is frozen for a turn.
🧱 Blocker — pick any tile on the main track; nobody (except a Shadow Phase token) can land on it for 2 full rounds.
⏪ Time Rewind — rewinds the whole board to how it looked 60 seconds ago.
🎯 Dice Hack — before rolling, pick your dice value directly instead of leaving it to chance.
🔃 Gravity Flip — reverses everyone's movement direction for 2 full rounds.
🌑 Shadow Phase — one of your tokens ignores Trap tiles and Blocker walls for 3 turns.

Rolling a 6, capturing an opponent, or getting a token home grants an extra roll. Three 6s in a row forfeits the turn. Star-marked cells are safe — no captures there.
